⚡

ENERGY
🌤️

MOOD
🧠

FOCUS

Strength & balance

Activities such as chair-to-stand and balance practice help you practise useful movement skills.

SKILLS

PAGE 04 · A MESSAGE FOR EVERY TEENAGER

Know your worth.
You matter.

Your value is not a grade, a number, a follower count, a body shape, a sports result, or someone else’s opinion. You are a person who is learning, growing, and figuring things out — and you deserve respect right now, not only after you achieve something.

01 · You don’t have to compare.

Everyone has a different story, pace, strengths, and challenges. Someone else’s success does not make your own progress smaller.

02 · Beginners belong here.

You do not need to be sporty, flexible, or confident to start. Trying something new is already a brave step.

03 · Talk to yourself with respect.

Instead of “I can’t do anything,” try “I’m still learning.” The words you use with yourself matter.

04 · Rest is not failure.

Taking a break, asking for help, or choosing an easier option is a smart way to care for yourself.

05 · Your voice matters.

Your ideas, feelings, boundaries, kindness, and creativity matter — even on days when you don’t feel confident.

06 · You deserve support.

If you are struggling, talk with someone you trust: a friend, family member, teacher, coach, or school counsellor.

YOU ARE WORTH IT. YOUR STORY IS STILL GROWING. ✦

Start small. Be kind to yourself. Let your inner fire grow.

WHAT REIGNITE REALLY MEANS

Find your inner
fire again.

REIGNITE means to light a fire again. For us, it means feeling the real energy, curiosity, imagination, and joy you had in childhood — when you wanted to explore, learn, play, and believe that wonderful things were possible.

As teenagers, it is easy to compare ourselves with others or feel that we are not good enough. But you do not need to be perfect, popular, sporty, or confident every day to have worth. You already matter, exactly as you are.

REIGNITE is a reminder to begin again in small ways: move in a way you enjoy, discover a new interest, rest when you need to, speak kindly to yourself, and spend time with people who support you. These small choices can help you build healthier habits, regain confidence, and make room for a happier, more meaningful life.

You do not have to become someone else. You can reconnect with the bright, curious person inside you and let that energy grow. Your story is still beginning. Your inner fire is still there. It is time to REIGNITE.
